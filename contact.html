Before You Write

Quick Answers

Can I submit a guest article?

Yes — we welcome well-researched guest posts from shelters, trainers, vets and rescue volunteers. Include a short outline and your credentials in your message.

Do you offer advertising or sponsorships?

We partner selectively with brands that are genuinely pet-friendly and ethical. Send us your proposal and we will review it.

I need urgent veterinary help — can you advise?

No. PawTales provides general educational content only. For urgent pet health concerns, please contact your veterinarian or an emergency animal hospital immediately.
