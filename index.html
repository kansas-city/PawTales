Frequently Asked Questions

Is PawTales content written by qualified experts?

Yes. Our articles are researched and reviewed with input from veterinary professionals, certified trainers, and experienced rescue volunteers before publishing.

How often is the rescue newsletter sent?

Our newsletter goes out twice a month with adoption spotlights, rescue success stories, care tips, and volunteer opportunities. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Can I contribute a rescue story or guest article?

Absolutely. We welcome guest posts from shelters, fosters, and pet professionals. Reach out through our contact page with your idea.

Do you recommend pet cleaning or home care products?

We only recommend products and services we have personally vetted. Our partnership with Xtreme Cleans is based on their pet-safe, Green Seal certified approach to cleaning.
