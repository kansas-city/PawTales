Who We Are

About PawTales

We are a community of pet owners, foster carers, and animal welfare advocates on a mission to make life better for pets — and the people who love them.

Our Story

PawTales began in a small living room where rescue volunteers traded stories about the dogs and cats nobody wanted — and the families who gave them a second chance. What started as a printed shelter newsletter grew into a full online resource for pet owners everywhere.

Today we publish practical, vet-informed articles, spotlight adoptable pets, and connect thousands of readers with the shelters that need them most. Every word we write has one goal: healthier, happier pets in loving homes.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower pet owners with trustworthy advice and champion animal rescue through storytelling and community.

💚

Our Values

Compassion, accuracy, transparency, and a firm belief that every animal deserves a safe and loving home.

🤝

Our Promise

Every article is researched and reviewed. We never publish advice we would not follow for our own pets.
