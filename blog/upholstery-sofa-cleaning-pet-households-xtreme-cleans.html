What's Hiding in Your Sofa

Even a tidy home collects a remarkable amount inside sofa cushions. Pet households see it faster than anyone:

  • Pet dander — microscopic skin flakes that trigger allergies and cling to fabric.
  • Fur and hair — woven deep into the weave, not just on the surface.
  • Saliva and drool — invisible but odour-forming over time.
  • Dirt and oils — carried in from paws and fur after every walk.
  • Odour molecules — trapped in foam and padding where a household spray can't reach.

Vacuuming helps, but it only touches the surface. The real grime lives deeper — which is exactly why professional extraction matters.

Why Vacuuming Isn't Enough

Regular vacuuming removes loose debris, but it cannot:

  • Extract oils and sticky residues bonded to the fabric.
  • Neutralise odour molecules trapped in the cushion foam.
  • Flush out allergens embedded below the surface.
  • Restore the fabric's fresh structure and appearance.

Left long enough, these build into that unmistakable "pet smell" that guests notice the moment they walk in — even when the room looks spotless.

The Xtreme Cleans Approach

Xtreme Cleans LLC treats upholstery as a specialist job, not an afterthought. Their method:

  • Fabric testing first. Every sofa is tested so the right low-moisture method and chemistry are used for delicate weaves, linen, and microfibre.
  • Truck-mounted hot water extraction. Powerful suction draws out dirt, dander and odour from cushions and frame.
  • Green Seal (GS-37) certified solutions. Biodegradable and safe for kids and pets — no harsh residues left behind for paws to pick up.
  • Dry in 2–4 hours. Your sofa is back in use the same day, not overnight.

"People are amazed what comes out of a sofa that looks clean. It's not just appearance — it's the air your family and pets breathe." — the Xtreme Cleans team

For pet households, cleaning the sofa and the carpet together delivers the biggest improvement, because odours travel between both surfaces and settle into the room's air.

How often should pet owners clean their upholstery?

Most pet households benefit from professional upholstery cleaning every 6 to 12 months, and more often if someone has allergies or the pet sheds heavily.
