Senior Pet Care: Helping Your Older Dog or Cat Thrive

✍️ Dr. Amara Fields📅 September 19, 2026⏱️ 7 min read

Age is not a disease. With a few thoughtful changes, senior pets can enjoy excellent quality of life well into their golden years.

When Is a Pet "Senior"?

PetSenior From
Small dogs~8 years
Large dogs~6–7 years
Cats~10 years

Adjust the Diet

  • Senior formulas support joints, kidneys and digestion.
  • Watch weight — obesity worsens arthritis and heart strain.
  • Increase hydration, especially for cats prone to kidney issues.

Support Mobility

  • Provide ramps or steps to reach favourite spots.
  • Use orthopaedic bedding for joint comfort.
  • Keep nails trimmed and consider non-slip mats on hard floors.
  • Switch to gentler, shorter, more frequent walks.

Offer Comfort and Warmth

Older pets feel the cold more. Give them warm, draft-free sleeping spots and easy access to food, water and litter trays on one level.

Keep the Mind Active

Gentle enrichment — sniff games, food puzzles, short training refreshers — keeps older brains sharp and lifts mood.

More Frequent Vet Visits

Twice-yearly check-ups help catch conditions early: arthritis, dental disease, kidney decline, thyroid issues and heart problems are all manageable when found soon. Watch for changes in appetite, thirst, urination, mobility and behaviour.

Older pets are not less deserving of joy — they are simply asking us to adapt with them. Small changes deliver enormous comfort.

This article is for general information only and is not a substitute for veterinary advice.
